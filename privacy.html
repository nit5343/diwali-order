Privacy

Diwali Group Order is a small page for a group of friends to put together one combined cracker order.

When you sign in with Google, the page receives your email address and name from Google. Your email and your cart (products, quantities, note, time of last change) are stored in a private Google Sheet owned by the organizer. Other signed-in members of the group can see your display name and what is in your cart, but not your email.

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else. The organizer deletes the data after the order is placed. To have your data removed earlier, contact the organizer.
